## before (after tpro-local alone)
- First post-MWL userspace write abort: esr …047 (translation L3) at
  PC = slid dyld VA 0x7bf44 `stp x8,x9,[x5]`, FAR = pool from
  MemoryManager::vm_map (often size 0x20000). vm_map returns KERN_SUCCESS
  but the VA has no L3 PTE; DSC RW subcaches (.33/.70/.81) are L2-miss.
- Guest RX at the fault PC matched **stock** `/usr/lib/dyld`, not the
  shared-cache embedded copy in `dyld_shared_cache_arm64e.01` (same VA
  offset 0x7bf44 / preferred 0x180198f44). Patching only .01 left guest
  phys at the PC as `stp`.

## patch
tools/patch_fullroot_dyld_alloc_page.py on /usr/lib/dyld:
  VA 0x7bf44 `stp x8,x9,[x5]` → `b 0x7bf68` (epilogue `mov x0,x19`)

Unit: tests/test-patch-fullroot-dyld-alloc-page.py

## after
- No FAULT with page-off 0x3f44 / ALLOC_SITE.
- OS_REASON still ns=2 code=0xb.
- Next write aborts are permission faults (esr …04f) into dyld
  `__DATA_CONST` (e.g. FAR unslid ~0xd8018), PC around dyld VA 0x7f960
  (see gdb-dyld-alloc-page.txt). Half-initialized Allocator after the
  freelist skip.
