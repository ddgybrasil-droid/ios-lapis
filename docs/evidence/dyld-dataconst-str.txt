## before (after alloc-page skip alone)
- OS_REASON still ns=2 code=0xb.
- First write-permission faults (esr …04f) were mis-attributed to dyld
  VA 0x7f960 by matching page offsets; live phys at the fault PC is:

    /usr/lib/dyld VA 0x7960  str x21, [x19]
    x19 / FAR in relocated __DATA_CONST (RO)

## patch
tools/patch_fullroot_dyld_dataconst_str.py:
  VA 0x7960 `str x21,[x19]` → `nop` on /usr/lib/dyld and .01 embedded dyld

Also ships tools/patch_fullroot_dyld_alloc_pool.py: redirect Allocator
bump-pool into __DATA_DIRTY 0xb9000 (completes freelist init instead of
the #17 epilogue skip).

## after
- No write fault with page-off matching 0x7960 / SITE7960.
- OS_REASON still ns=2 code=0xb.
- Next write aborts include dyld VA 0x3288 `str x20,[x8,#0x308]` into
  slid __DATA 0xb4308 (near the TPRO local-store cave) and later sites
  (see gdb-dyld-dataconst-str.txt).
