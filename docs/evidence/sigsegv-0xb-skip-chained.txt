## before (after PAC strip alone)
- MOUNTED → MWL → OS_REASON ns=2 code=0xb
- GDB data abort IFSC=7 at dyld chained-fixup walker:
  PC unslid 0x2b5d0 `ldr x25,[x21]`, LR 0x2b408 (caller after `bl 0x2b564`)
  FAR = launchd __DATA + page_start 0x18 (ptrfmt 12 / USERLAND24)
- launchd chained starts: __DATA page[0] start=0x18; __DATA_CONST pages start=0

## patch
tools/patch_fullroot_dyld_skip_chained.py on /usr/lib/dyld:

1. Walker VA 0x2b564 → `mov w0,#0; ret`
   Leave USERLAND24 fixups to Image.ipados Auth64 dyld pager (page-in).

2. Header walk VA 0x652f4 `b.cc` → `b` (always take old-header early exit)
   Without (1), boot never reached this. With (1) alone, next SEGV was:
   `ldr w9,[x21,#4]` at VA 0x65324 with
   x21 = *(cache_base+0x208)+slide (on-disk preferred 0x2b925fae0) —
   inside sharedRegionSize but unmapped (subcache hole).

Unit: tests/test-patch-fullroot-dyld-skip-chained.py

## after
- GDB: first userspace data abort is no longer the 0x2b5d0 walker or 0x65324
  header walk. New wall: write translation fault (esr …047) FAR in shared
  cache (e.g. 0x1ef9b0040), PC still in dyld/main ASLR range.
- OS_REASON still ns=2 code=0xb (SIGSEGV) — same signal class, new site.
- msgbuf: `pid 1 exited -- exit reason namespace 2 subcode 0xb`
  (docs/evidence/msgbuf-sigsegv-0xb-skip.txt)
- gdb: docs/evidence/gdb-sigsegv-0xb-skip.txt
